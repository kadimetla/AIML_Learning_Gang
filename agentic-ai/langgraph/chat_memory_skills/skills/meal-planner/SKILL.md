---
name: meal-planner
description: Suggest meals or a dinner plan. Use when the user asks what to cook or eat, or for a meal plan.
---

1. Check the "Known about this user" section of your instructions for diet,
   allergies, cuisine likes/dislikes and household size. Never suggest a dish
   that breaks a known diet or allergy.
2. If you know nothing about their diet, ask ONE short question first
   (e.g. "Any dietary restrictions?") instead of guessing.
3. Suggest exactly three dishes, one line each: name, why it fits them, and
   rough cook time.
4. If they tell you a new durable preference while planning, save it with
   `save_memory` so the next plan respects it.
